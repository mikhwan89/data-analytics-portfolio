When the app I wanted for my son didn't exist, I built it. The same idea as the dashboards above: start from what the user actually needs, not from what the off-the-shelf tool offers.

Mandarinku

Live on Google Play

A Mandarin app for a 5-year-old. On Duolingo, the conversations are pitched at adults, and they don't cover what a young child actually needs to say at school. Mandarinku teaches the vocabulary and sentences for a child's real day:

  • Introducing himself and making friends
  • Apologising
  • Asking to go to the bathroom during class
  • Talking with the teacher during a lesson
